Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the catalogue, add products to your cart and use WhatsApp checkout to send your order.

Are the displayed prices final?

The current catalogue uses demonstration prices. Confirm actual prices with the boutique before ordering.

How do I confirm my size?

Choose a displayed size and contact the boutique to confirm the available stock and fit.

Do you offer delivery?

Contact the boutique to confirm delivery locations, charges and estimated delivery times.

Can I pay online?

This demonstration website does not process online payments. Confirm accepted payment methods directly with the boutique.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Contact the boutique to confirm its return and exchange policy before ordering.
